How the SPIFF works

Five things to know before you apply. This page is the full set of terms: the short version comes first, the detail is at the end.

✓

Paid per post that counts

We pay a fixed amount for each original piece of content about Amplemarket that is live and meets the rules. We pay for content, never for views, likes, comments or clicks.

✓

50 seats, by application

Apply by Friday, October 30 at 12:00 UTC. We accept up to 50 partners.

✓

One month to post

Publish between November 1 and November 30, 2026 on LinkedIn, X, YouTube, TikTok, or your own blog or newsletter, from the accounts you applied with.

✓

It counts, or it doesn't

We review every Friday. No partial payouts, no draft reviews, no revisions, and we always tell you why. If a post doesn't count, you can publish a new one.

✓

Stacks on top

The SPIFF is extra. Your usual opportunity bonus and commission still apply to deals that come through your SPIFF link.

What you earn

Two tiers, up to 5 posts per partner, paid through PartnerStack with your usual commission.

✓

Standard → $75 per post

Any post that meets every rule on this page.

✓

Featured → $200 per post

Also shows the product in use (demo video, screen recording or a 5+ slide walkthrough, with data blurred), or shares your own measurable result, or is a full blog post or newsletter edition. You can request a tier; we set the final tier.

✓

Up to 5 posts

At least 5 days apart. The same content posted on several channels counts once.

✓

Paid through PartnerStack

Every Friday, we email you the decision on each post, its tier and your running total. Approved posts are issued as a reward in PartnerStack on the 1st of the month after approval and paid out alongside your usual commission. Posts approved in November are issued on December 1.

What counts

A post counts only if it meets all of these.

✓

Original and new

Published Nov 1-30, 2026. Edited older posts and re-uploaded videos don't count.

✓

Public

Visible to anyone on the site or app where you post (LinkedIn, X, YouTube, TikTok or the open web), not only to an audience you choose. That rules out LinkedIn posts set to "Connections only", X Circles or protected accounts, unlisted or private YouTube videos, TikTok posts set to "Friends" or "Only me", and subscriber-only newsletters with no public web version.

✓

About Amplemarket

Amplemarket is the main subject, not a passing mention. For video, the whole video is about Amplemarket, not one segment of a video about several tools. Priority topics: Duo Copilot, the Parallel Dialer, the MCP, CRM Enrichment and Duo Accounts.

✓

Substance

At least one of: a workflow you run, a result you got (your own numbers), a before/after, a how-to, a demo, or an opinion with reasoning, or content built on the content kit we send accepted partners.

✓

Minimum format

Written posts (LinkedIn, X): 50+ words; a thread counts as one post. Video: 30+ seconds. Carousel: 5+ slides. Blog, article or newsletter: 400+ words.

✓

Your SPIFF link

We create a personal tracking link for you in your PartnerStack account. Put it in the post, the video description or the first comment; on TikTok, in your bio while the post is up. Don't shorten or edit it: that strips your tracking code. Links sent by DM, or "comment X and I'll send it", don't count.

✓

Tag us

On X, tag the Amplemarket account. On LinkedIn, write "Amplemarket" or "Amplemarket.com".

✓

Your language

English, Portuguese, Spanish, French or German: the languages our team can review.

✓

Submitted within 7 days

Through the submission form, with the post URL. The last day to submit November posts is December 7, 2026.

✓

Live for 60 days

The post must be public when we review it and on payout day, and stay up for 60 days. If a paid post is removed earlier, we claw back the payout from your future commissions.

✓

What doesn't count

Reshares, reposts, comments or link-only posts. Posts mainly about another tool. Roundups and listicles of several tools, even if Amplemarket is ranked first. Templated text, or copies of another partner's post or of our example posts. Generic AI output with no specifics. Posts in closed groups or private accounts. Engagement bait.

Red lines

Write in your own voice: we don't pre-review content. We only step in when a post crosses one of these. If it does, the post doesn't count and we ask you to take it down.

Repeated or deliberate breaches can end your participation in the SPIFF.

1

Invented results or guarantees

Only your own numbers, stated as yours, with context. Nothing like "2x your pipeline".

2

Prospect or customer data on screen

Blur names, emails, phone numbers and companies in every screenshot and video.

3

Attacks on competitors

Factual, first-hand comparisons are fine. Name-and-shame isn't.

4

Pricing, discounts or roadmap

Never quote prices, discounts or unreleased features. If people ask about pricing, send them to your SPIFF link, where they can book a demo or start a free trial.

Yes: by submitting, you let us reshare, quote and feature your approved content, credited to you. We'll ask before using it in paid ads.

Will the SPIFF run past November?

Maybe. If we extend it, we'll tell the cohort in writing before December 1.

Full terms

Version 1, published October 21, 2026. These terms sit alongside your Amplemarket partner agreement and PartnerStack program terms, which still apply. If anything here conflicts with your partner agreement, your partner agreement wins.

1

What the SPIFF is

Amplemarket pays a fixed amount for each original piece of content about Amplemarket that is live, published in the content window, and meets these terms. We pay for content, never for views, likes, comments or clicks.

2

Who can take part

The SPIFF is open by application to active Amplemarket partners. Applications close October 30, 2026 at 12:00 UTC. We accept up to 50 partners and send decisions by October 30, 2026. Acceptance is at Amplemarket's discretion. Only content published from the accounts and channels listed in your application counts; accounts created for this SPIFF do not.

3

Content window

Content must be published between November 1 and November 30, 2026 (inclusive, any time zone) and submitted through the submission form within 7 days of publishing. The last day to submit November content is December 7, 2026. Amplemarket may extend the window into December; if it does, it will tell the cohort in writing before December 1.

4

What qualifies

A post counts only if it meets all of the requirements in "What counts" above: original and new, on an eligible channel, public, about Amplemarket, with substance, meeting the minimum format, with your SPIFF link (created for you in your PartnerStack account), tagged as described, in an accepted language, within the cap and spacing, and submitted through the form.

5

What doesn't qualify

Reshares, reposts, comments or link-only posts; posts mainly about another tool; roundups and listicles of several tools, even if Amplemarket is ranked first; templated or near-identical text to your earlier posts or another partner's posts, including copies of our example posts; generic, low-effort AI output; posts in closed groups, private communities or private accounts; engagement bait with no standalone value; anything that crosses a red line.

6

Tiers and payouts

Standard: $75 per post that meets every requirement. Featured: $200 per post that also shows the product in use (with data blurred), or shares the partner's own measurable result, or is a full blog post or newsletter edition. Up to 5 posts per partner, at least 5 days apart. You may request a tier; Amplemarket sets the final tier. Your existing opportunity bonus and commission apply to opportunities created through your SPIFF link under the current attribution rules; accounts that are already open opportunities or in active Amplemarket sequences aren't credited.

7

Review and decisions

We review submissions every Friday in November and the first two Fridays of December. Each post either counts or doesn't: no partial payouts, no draft reviews, no revisions. The post must be live and public when reviewed. If a post doesn't count, we tell you why, and you can publish a new one within your cap. Review decisions are final.

8

Payment

Every Friday, we email you the decision on each post we reviewed that week, its tier and your running total. Approved posts are issued as a reward in PartnerStack on the 1st of the month after approval and paid out alongside your usual commission. Posts approved in November are issued on December 1; posts approved in December, on January 1. Keep your payout details in PartnerStack up to date so nothing gets held up. You're responsible for any taxes on SPIFF payments.

9

Brand guidelines and red lines

Write "Amplemarket" as one word with a capital A; use product names as they appear on amplemarket.com; use only logos and screenshots from our brand assets; describe yourself as an Amplemarket partner, not as someone who works for or speaks for Amplemarket. A post that crosses a red line doesn't count, and we ask you to take it down. Repeated or deliberate breaches can end your participation in the SPIFF (see General).

10

Keep it live

Approved content must stay live and public for 60 days after publishing. We check that approved posts are still live on payout day; a post that isn't live on payout day isn't paid. If a paid post is removed within 60 days, we claw back the payout from your future commissions.

11

Content rights

By submitting, you allow Amplemarket to reshare, quote, link to and feature your approved content, credited to you, on its own channels and in partner and sales materials. We'll ask your permission before using your content in paid ads.

12

Disclosure

You're responsible for following the advertising disclosure rules that apply to you, your audience and the platform you post on. Many countries expect paid endorsements to be labelled. We recommend a short disclosure such as "#ad", "Paid partnership with Amplemarket" or your local equivalent, and switching on the platform's paid-promotion setting where one exists. Disclosure doesn't affect your payout.

13

Changes and ending the SPIFF

Amplemarket may change or end the SPIFF at any time by telling the cohort in writing. Content approved before that notice is paid under the terms that applied when it was published.

14

General

Taking part doesn't make you an employee, agent or representative of Amplemarket. Amplemarket may remove a partner for breaking these terms, for fraud (including fake accounts, bought engagement or recycled content), or for conduct that harms Amplemarket's reputation. Content approved before removal is paid unless it involved fraud.
